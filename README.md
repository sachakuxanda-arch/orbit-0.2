# Kernel 001 · Orbit · K2 Iterated · v0.3

> An experiment in allowing structure to emerge from thinking.

Most systems ask you to organise your thoughts before you have accumulated enough of them to know what matters.

Orbit takes the opposite approach.

**Think first. Accumulate. Notice what emerges.**

---

## What is Orbit?

Orbit is a small, local-first experiment in **structured thinking**.

You bring something you are carrying — a decision, a fear, a belief, an opportunity, a question, or something you cannot quite put down.

That is your **signal**.

You then choose a **Loop Lens** through which to examine it.

The lens changes the questions.

The purpose is not to produce an answer.

It is to make the **structure of your thinking more visible**.

Orbit is not a quiz, personality test, productivity tool, or prediction system.

It is a practice.

---

## The emerging architecture

Orbit is beginning to separate things that are often collapsed together:

### Signal

**What you are carrying.**

The signal is the thing being examined.

It can change from loop to loop.

### Loop Lens

**How you choose to examine it.**

Different lenses expose different structures within the same signal.

The current prototype introduces:

* Fear
* Decision
* Opportunity
* Belief

Only **Fear** is currently active in Kernel 001.

The others represent the direction of the architecture rather than completed functionality.

### Kernel

**The structured workflow that performs the examination.**

A Kernel is not simply a collection of questions.

It is a workflow that can be tested, iterated, transferred and eventually validated through use.

### Engine

**The underlying learning loop through which reasoning, outcomes and iteration accumulate.**

The architecture is deliberately small at this stage.

The intention is to **discover what should become structure through use**, rather than designing a complete taxonomy in advance.

---

## The learning loop

A single Orbit session is only one point in a larger process:

**Signal → Reasoning → Assumptions → Uncertainty → Judgment → Outcome → Comparison → Update → Next loop**

The first part of the loop is structured reflection.

The second part happens when reality responds.

A session initially becomes an **open loop**.

The user can return later and record:

* What actually happened
* What changed in their thinking

The loop can then be marked **closed**.

This matters because the system is not designed merely to capture what someone thought.

It is designed to capture **what happened to that thinking when it encountered reality**.

> **The loop closes.
> The orbit continues from a different position.**

## Current version

### Orbit 0.3 — Kernel 001

Version 0.3 introduces the first visible separation between:

* the **signal** being examined
* the **lens** used to examine it
* the **Kernel** performing the workflow
* the **Orbit engine** running the learning loop
* the **provenance** of the process

The current prototype includes:

* an editable personal signal
* a Loop Lens
* approximately five minutes to complete

* return-to-reality loop closure

* a visual representation of accumulated thinking
* user-defined names for areas of thinking

* engine/version information
* no account
* no server-side storage

The prototype remains intentionally small.

**The architecture is evolving through use.**

## Open loops and reality

One of the changes in 0.3 is that a thinking session does not have to pretend to be complete.

After the initial loop, the user can keep the record locally and allow reality to respond.

The session remains:

> **Open — awaiting reality**

When something happens, the user can return and record:

> **What actually happened?**

and:

> **What changed in your thinking?**

The session then becomes:

> **Closed**

This creates the beginning of a longer-term learning record.

The value is not simply remembering what you thought.

It is being able to compare **what you thought with what happened**.

---

## Personal accumulation

Orbit is beginning to treat repeated thinking as something that can accumulate.

Each retained loop can contain:

* the original signal
* the Loop Lens
* the answers
* a user-defined domain
* an intensity/depth measure
* its current state
* its eventual outcome
* what was learned

The local library provides a visual record of that accumulation.

The intention is not to score a person.

It is to make **patterns in their own thinking increasingly visible to them**.

---

## Provenance

Orbit is designed around a simple principle:

> **Your thinking has provenance.**

Every retained loop carries information about where the reasoning process came from.

The current provenance record identifies:

* Kernel
* Kernel maturity
* Signal
* Loop Lens
* Engine version
* Origin

This makes the evolution of the practice traceable.

The system should eventually make it possible to understand **where a workflow came from, how it changed, what was learned through its use, and why it became trusted.**

At the personal level, reasoning belongs to the person who produced it.

At the system level, validated learning can contribute to something larger.

The distinction matters.

> **The person owns their experience.
> The network can benefit from what they choose to contribute.**

---

## Export

Orbit allows the user to take their reasoning with them.

A retained or completed session can be copied or downloaded as a structured text record containing:

* Kernel provenance
* signal
* domain
* Loop Lens
* questions and answers
* iteration information
* observations to be added when reality responds

The record is designed to remain understandable outside the interface.

**The thinking belongs to the person, not the screen.**

## What we're exploring

Can repeated moments of honest thinking reveal patterns that are difficult to see in isolation?

Can those patterns become more meaningful as they accumulate?

Can different lenses reveal different structures within the same signal?

Can returning to an earlier thought after reality has responded produce better learning?

Can tested workflows become reusable **Kernels**?

Can provenance make those workflows more trustworthy?

And eventually:

> **Can human reasoning become the raw material from which new ways of thinking and working emerge?**

For now, we are testing the smallest useful version of that idea.

## Status

**K2 — Iterated**

Development and iteration are recorded through the repository history.

* Fear is the active Loop Lens.
