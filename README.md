# Orbit
0.2 This version experimental - in tandem with the Linkedin launch.

### An experiment in allowing structure to emerge from thinking.

Most systems ask you to organise your thoughts before you have accumulated enough of them to know what matters.

Orbit takes the opposite approach.

**Think first.
Accumulate.
Notice what emerges.**

Orbit is a small, local-first experiment in structured thinking. It gives a person a short reflection loop, allows sessions to accumulate, and lets them begin to recognise the threads running through their own thinking.

There is no prescribed taxonomy.

When a pattern begins to feel meaningful, the person gets to decide what to call it.

> **If this thinking had a name, what would you call it?**

That name belongs to the thinker.

Orbit is not trying to tell people what to think.

It is an experiment in helping people see **what their thinking becomes when it is allowed to accumulate.**

## Current version

**Orbit 0.2 — Kernel 001**

This version is intentionally small.

It currently provides:

* a five-question reflection loop
* optional local persistence
* a personal session history
* a visual representation of accumulated thinking
* user-defined domain names
* local export
* no account
* no server-side storage

This is an experiment, not a finished system.

The purpose of 0.2 is to put the practice into people's hands and observe what happens.

## What we're exploring

Can repeated moments of honest thinking reveal patterns that are difficult to see in isolation?

Can those patterns become more meaningful as they accumulate?

And eventually:

**Can a person's own reasoning become the raw material from which new ways of thinking and working emerge?**

For now, we're simply starting with the first step:

**Think. Then come back.**
